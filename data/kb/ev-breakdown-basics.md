---
id: ev-breakdown-basics
title: Electric vehicle breakdown basics
category: safety
---

If an electric vehicle stops, put it in Park, switch on hazard lights and move people away from traffic.

Do not open or touch high-voltage parts, usually marked with orange cables.

Some electric vehicles should not be towed with the drive wheels on the ground. Tell the mechanic or tow operator that it is an EV so they bring a flatbed.

If you see smoke or smell burning from an EV, move well away and call 112.
