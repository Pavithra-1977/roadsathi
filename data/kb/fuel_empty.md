---
id: fuel_empty
title: Out of fuel
category: fault
---

Signs: the engine sputters and stops, fuel gauge on empty, reserve light was on. Words drivers use: petrol khatam, diesel khatam, fuel over, tank empty, reserve.

Do not keep cranking the starter. It drains the battery and can damage the fuel pump.

A mechanic or fuel stop can bring fuel in an approved can. Diesel vehicles may need the fuel system primed or bled after running dry, which a mechanic can do.

Never carry fuel inside the passenger cabin, and do not smoke or use open flames near the vehicle while refuelling.
