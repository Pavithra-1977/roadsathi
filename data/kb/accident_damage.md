---
id: accident_damage
title: Collision or accident damage
category: fault
---

After any collision: if anyone may be hurt, call 108 (ambulance) or 112 first, before anything else.

Switch off the engine, switch on hazard lights, and move uninjured people away from traffic. Do not move an injured person unless there is an immediate danger such as fire.

Do not drive a vehicle after a collision until it has been checked, even if it looks fine. Leaking fluids, damaged steering or a damaged wheel may not be visible.

Take photos of the scene and the vehicles only when it is safe to do so. The vehicle will usually need towing.
