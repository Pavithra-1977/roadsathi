---
id: highway-shoulder-position
title: Positioning the vehicle on a highway shoulder
category: safety
---

Stop as far left as the shoulder allows, with the wheels turned away from traffic.

Avoid stopping on bends, bridges, hill crests or just after a toll plaza merge, where other drivers have little time to see you. If the vehicle can still move slowly and safely, continue to a wider spot or a lay-by.

Apply the handbrake and leave the vehicle in gear (or Park).

Everyone should exit on the side away from traffic. Children first.
