---
id: fan_belt
title: Fan belt or serpentine belt snapped
category: fault
---

Signs: a loud squeal or slap, then the battery light and temperature warning come on, power steering may become heavy. Words drivers use: belt toot gaya, fan belt, squealing, screech.

The belt often drives the water pump and alternator. Without it the engine will overheat and the battery will run down.

Switch off and do not restart. A mechanic can usually fit a new belt at the roadside if the pulleys and tensioner are not damaged.
