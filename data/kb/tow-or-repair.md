title: Tow or repair: how the decision is made
category: decision
---

A roadside repair makes sense when the fault is simple, the parts are available nearby, and the work can be done safely away from traffic. Examples: punctures, flat batteries, a fuse, a slipped chain, running out of fuel.

Towing is the safer choice when the fault affects brakes, steering, suspension or the engine itself, after any collision, or when the work needs the vehicle lifted for a long time.

If the mechanic says a repair is not safe at the roadside, accept that advice. A tow costs less than a second breakdown or an accident.
