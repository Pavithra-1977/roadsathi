title: Family and child safety on highways
category: safety
---

Children should be the first people moved away from the vehicle and the last to return. Hold small children by the hand and keep them on the side away from traffic.

Never leave children alone in a parked vehicle, especially in heat or with the engine off and windows closed.

Keep water, a torch and something warm handy. Reassure children with a simple plan: we wait here, help is coming.

If you have small children and the vehicle cannot be fixed quickly, consider safe onward travel or a nearby lodge rather than waiting for hours at the roadside.
