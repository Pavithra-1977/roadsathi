title: Truck and heavy vehicle breakdown
category: safety
---

Heavy vehicles take up much of the shoulder, so visibility matters even more.

Use hazard lights, parking lights and reflectors. Place warning triangles well behind and, if possible, ahead of the vehicle.

Never work under a raised heavy vehicle without proper stands. Many faults on heavy vehicles need specialist recovery equipment.

The driver and any helpers should wait away from the vehicle, behind the barrier.
