---
id: battery_dead
title: Dead battery or no crank
category: fault
---

Signs: the engine does not turn over, you hear only clicking, dashboard lights are dim or dead. Words drivers use: battery dead, self nahi le raha, not starting, gaadi start nahi ho rahi, click click.

Switch off AC, music and headlights so any remaining charge is kept for the restart. Keep hazard lights on for visibility; they draw little power.

A mechanic can usually jump-start the vehicle or fit a new battery at the roadside. If the battery dies again soon after a jump start, the charging system (alternator) may be the real cause.

Do not attempt a jump start yourself unless you know the correct connection order. See the jump-start safety guide.
