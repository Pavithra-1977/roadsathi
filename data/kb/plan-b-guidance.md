id: plan-b-guidance
title: Plan B: getting people home when the vehicle cannot be fixed
category: plan-b
---

When the vehicle cannot be repaired tonight, the priority changes from the vehicle to the people.

Options to consider: a verified cab, a bus from a staffed bus station, a train from the nearest station, or a family-safe lodge if children are travelling.

Before leaving the vehicle: remove valuables and documents, take photos of all sides and the odometer, and get a written or digital receipt from whoever takes custody of the vehicle.

Share your onward travel details and the cab plate number with a family member.
