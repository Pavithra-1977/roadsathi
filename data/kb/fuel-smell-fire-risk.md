id: fuel-smell-fire-risk
title: Fuel smell, smoke or fire risk
category: safety
---

If you smell fuel, see smoke from under the bonnet, or see flames: switch off the engine, get everyone out immediately and move well away from the vehicle, upwind if possible.

Call 112. Do not open the bonnet if you suspect a fire; it can feed the fire with air.

Do not return to the vehicle to collect belongings.

Do not smoke or use a lighter near a vehicle that smells of fuel.
