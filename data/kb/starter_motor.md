---
id: starter_motor
title: Starter motor failure
category: fault
---

Signs: a single click or whirring sound when you turn the key, but the lights and dashboard work normally. Words drivers use: self start nahi, self not working, engine not turning.

If the lights are bright and only the starter fails, the battery is probably fine.

A mechanic can test the starter and solenoid. Sometimes the starter can be replaced at the roadside. Do not try to push-start an automatic vehicle.
