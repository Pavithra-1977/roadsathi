---
id: when-not-to-repair
title: When not to attempt a repair yourself
category: decision
---

Do not attempt any repair yourself if:
- the vehicle is on a narrow shoulder, a bend, a bridge or close to moving traffic;
- you would need to lie under the vehicle or jack it on soft or sloping ground;
- there is smoke, a fuel smell, a burning smell or visible fluid leaking;
- the engine is hot and the fault involves the radiator or coolant;
- the brakes, steering or a wheel feel damaged;
- it is dark and you do not have proper lighting;
- you are unsure what is wrong.

In these cases keep everyone safe behind the barrier and wait for a trained mechanic.
