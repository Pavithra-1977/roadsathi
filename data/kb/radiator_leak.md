---
id: radiator_leak
title: Radiator or coolant hose leak
category: fault
---

Signs: green, pink or orange liquid dripping under the front, low coolant warning, rising temperature. Words drivers use: paani leak, coolant leak, radiator leak, puddle under car.

Switch off the engine and let it cool. Do not open the radiator cap while hot.

Coolant is harmful if swallowed. Keep children and animals away from the puddle.

A mechanic can often replace a split hose or clamp at the roadside and refill coolant. A cracked radiator usually needs a workshop.
