---
id: two-wheeler-breakdown
title: Two-wheeler breakdown safety
category: safety
---

Move the bike fully off the road and put it on its stand on firm ground.

Keep your helmet on while near traffic and wear light-coloured or reflective clothing if you have it.

Switch on hazard lights if your bike has them, or keep the tail light on.

Do not push a heavy bike along a busy highway shoulder for long distances. Wait in a safe spot for help.
