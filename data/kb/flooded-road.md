---
id: flooded-road
title: Water-logged or flooded roads
category: weather
---

Do not drive into water when you cannot see the road surface or how deep it is.

If the engine stops in water, do not try to restart it. Water drawn into the engine can cause serious damage.

If water is rising around the vehicle, leave the vehicle and move to higher ground. Do not stay inside a vehicle in moving water.

A stalled vehicle in water should be towed, not jump-started.
