---
id: chain_snap
title: Drive chain snapped or slipped (two-wheeler)
category: fault
---

Signs: the engine revs but the bike does not move, a rattle or slap, the chain hanging loose. Words drivers use: chain toot gayi, chain utar gayi, sprocket.

Move the bike off the road on its stand. Keep fingers away from the chain and sprocket if the engine is running.

A mechanic can usually refit a slipped chain or fit a new master link at the roadside. A badly worn chain and sprocket set should be replaced at a workshop soon after.
