---
id: spark_plug
title: Spark plug or ignition misfire
category: fault
---

Signs: jerking, sputtering, loss of power, the engine shaking at idle, a flashing check-engine light. Words drivers use: jhatke, misfire, engine shaking, pickup nahi.

Drive gently to the nearest safe stopping point. Continuing with a heavy misfire can damage other parts.

A mechanic can check and replace spark plugs or ignition coils at the roadside for most petrol vehicles. Diesel engines do not use spark plugs, so similar symptoms need a different check.
