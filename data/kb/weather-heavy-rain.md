id: weather-heavy-rain
title: Breakdown in heavy rain
category: weather
---

In heavy rain other drivers see you late and stopping distances are longer.

Move as far from the carriageway as possible. Keep hazard lights and parking lights on.

Wait behind the barrier rather than inside the vehicle where it is safe to do so, but avoid standing in moving water or under trees and power lines.

Do not attempt repairs on wet, sloping ground. Watch for water rising around the vehicle; if it does, move to higher ground.
