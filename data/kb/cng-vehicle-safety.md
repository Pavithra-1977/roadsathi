---
id: cng-vehicle-safety
title: CNG vehicle breakdown safety
category: safety
---

If you smell gas or hear a hissing sound in a CNG vehicle, switch off the engine, turn off the CNG cylinder valve if you know how, and move everyone away from the vehicle.

Do not smoke or use any flame nearby. Call 112 if you suspect a leak.

Only a trained CNG mechanic should work on the gas system. Many CNG vehicles can switch to petrol, but do not do this if you suspect a leak.
