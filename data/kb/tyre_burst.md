---
id: tyre_burst
title: Tyre burst or sidewall damage
category: fault
---

A burst is a sudden bang followed by loss of control or heavy pulling. Words drivers use: burst, blast, tyre phat gaya, bang, shredded, sidewall cut.

Hold the steering firmly, do not brake hard, and let the vehicle slow down before steering gently to the shoulder.

A burst tyre cannot be patched. It needs the spare wheel or a replacement tyre. Check whether the wheel arch, brake line or body has been damaged before driving again.

Do not drive on the rim, even for a short distance. Stay behind the barrier while you wait.
