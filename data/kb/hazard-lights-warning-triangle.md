title: Hazard lights and warning triangle
category: safety
---

Switch on hazard lights the moment you know you have a problem, before you stop.

Place the warning triangle well behind the vehicle in the same lane so approaching drivers have time to react. On a bend or a hill crest, place it before the bend or crest.

Walk to place the triangle facing oncoming traffic, on the side away from the road, and only if it is safe. If it is not safe, skip it and move away from the vehicle.

If you have no triangle, any reflective or bright object can help, but never risk your safety to place it.
