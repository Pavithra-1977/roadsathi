---
id: key_lockout
title: Keys locked in or lost key
category: fault
---

Signs: keys visible inside a locked vehicle, a lost key, or a remote or immobiliser that no longer works. Words drivers use: chabi andar reh gayi, key lost, lock ho gaya.

If a child or pet is locked inside, especially in heat, call 112 immediately. Do not wait for a mechanic.

Otherwise, a locksmith-trained mechanic can usually open the vehicle without damage. Keep proof of ownership ready; a responsible mechanic will ask for it.
