id: jump-start-safety
title: Jump-start safety
category: safety
---

Only jump-start if you know the correct order and the battery is not cracked, leaking or swollen.

General order: red to the dead battery positive, red to the good battery positive, black to the good battery negative, black to an unpainted metal point on the dead vehicle away from the battery. Remove in reverse order.

Keep sparks and flames away from batteries. Do not lean over the battery while connecting.

If you are unsure, wait for the mechanic. A wrong connection can damage electronics.
