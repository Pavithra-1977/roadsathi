---
id: flat_tyre
title: Flat tyre or puncture
category: fault
---

A flat tyre usually shows as the vehicle pulling to one side, a flapping sound, or a wheel sitting low. Common words drivers use: puncture, punchar, pancher, flat, tyre down, hawa nikal gayi.

Pull fully off the carriageway on firm, level ground before doing anything. Switch on hazard lights and get passengers out and behind the barrier.

Do not jack the vehicle on a soft shoulder, sand or a slope. If the ground is not firm, wait for the mechanic rather than trying yourself.

A mechanic can usually repair a tubeless puncture with a plug kit, or fit the spare wheel (stepney). Driving any distance on a fully flat tyre can damage the rim and make the repair more expensive.
