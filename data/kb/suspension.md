---
id: suspension
title: Suspension, ball joint or axle damage
category: fault
---

Signs: a loud clunk after a pothole, a wheel leaning at an odd angle, steering pulling hard, the vehicle sitting low on one corner. Words drivers use: shocker toota, pothole, wheel tedha, steering kheench raha.

Do not drive a vehicle with a wheel at an odd angle. A failed ball joint can cause the wheel to separate.

This is normally a tow job. A mechanic can assess whether the vehicle is safe to move at very low speed or needs a flatbed.
