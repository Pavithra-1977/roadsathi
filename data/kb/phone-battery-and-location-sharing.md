id: phone-battery-and-location-sharing
title: Phone battery and location sharing
category: safety
---

Your phone is your lifeline. Lower screen brightness, close unused apps and turn off mobile data on a second phone to keep it as a reserve.

Share your live location with a family member and send them the RoadSathi guardian link.

If mobile data is weak, an SMS can still get through. RoadSathi accepts an SOS by SMS with your coordinates.

Note a nearby landmark or kilometre stone in case your phone dies.
