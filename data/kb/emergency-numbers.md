id: emergency-numbers
title: Emergency numbers in India
category: emergency
---

112 - the national emergency number. Connects you to police, fire and ambulance. Works from any phone.

108 - ambulance service in most states. Call it first if anyone may be injured.

1033 - the national highway helpline, for incidents and assistance on national highways.

When you call, give: your location (highway number, nearest town or kilometre stone), what happened, how many people are with you, and whether anyone is hurt. Stay on the line if asked.
