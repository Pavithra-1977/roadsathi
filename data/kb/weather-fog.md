---
id: weather-fog
title: Breakdown in fog or low visibility
category: weather
---

In fog, a stopped vehicle can be very hard to see until other drivers are close.

Keep hazard lights and parking lights on. Place the warning triangle further back than usual if you can do so safely.

Get everyone out and well away from the road, behind a barrier if there is one. Do not stand behind the vehicle.

Wear or hold something light-coloured or reflective, and use a phone torch to make yourself visible if you have to move near the road.
