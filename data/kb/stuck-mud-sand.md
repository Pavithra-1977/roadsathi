---
id: stuck-mud-sand
title: Vehicle stuck in mud or sand
category: safety
---

Do not keep spinning the wheels. It digs the vehicle deeper and can damage the clutch or transmission.

Reduce speed to a gentle crawl, straighten the front wheels, and try moving slowly in a low gear.

Do not stand behind or beside a spinning wheel; debris can be thrown out.

If the vehicle does not move after a few gentle attempts, wait for recovery help.
