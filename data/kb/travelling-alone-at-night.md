id: travelling-alone-at-night
title: Travelling alone at night
category: safety
---

Share your live location and the details of your breakdown with a trusted person straight away.

If you feel unsafe at the roadside and the vehicle is in a protected place, you may choose to wait inside with doors locked and hazard lights on. On a busy highway shoulder, waiting behind the barrier is usually safer.

Be cautious about accepting help from strangers who stop. Verify any mechanic through the app before opening the vehicle.

If you feel threatened, call 112.
