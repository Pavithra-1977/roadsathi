title: Blown fuse or wiring fault
category: fault
---

Signs: one circuit stops working, such as headlights, indicators, horn or the fuel pump, or a burning plastic smell. Words drivers use: light nahi jal rahi, fuse ud gaya, wiring, short circuit.

If you smell burning plastic or see smoke from wiring, switch off the engine and move everyone away from the vehicle.

A mechanic can find and replace blown fuses and repair simple wiring faults at the roadside. Do not replace a fuse with a higher-rated one or a piece of wire.
