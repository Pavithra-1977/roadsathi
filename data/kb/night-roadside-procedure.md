id: night-roadside-procedure
title: Safe roadside procedure at night
category: safety
---

1. Signal early and move as far left off the carriageway as possible, ideally past a barrier or onto a wide, firm shoulder.
2. Switch on hazard lights and parking lights straight away.
3. Get every passenger out on the side away from traffic and stand behind the crash barrier or well away from the road.
4. Place a warning triangle, or any reflective object, well behind the vehicle. Only do this if you can walk there safely.
5. Do not stand between the vehicle and traffic, and do not sit inside a stopped vehicle on a highway shoulder.
6. Share your live location with someone you trust and keep your phone charged.
