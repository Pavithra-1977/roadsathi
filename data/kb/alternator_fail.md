id: alternator_fail
title: Alternator or charging system failure
category: fault
---

Signs: battery warning light comes on while driving, lights dim or flicker, electrics fail one by one, then the engine stops. Words drivers use: battery light, charging nahi ho raha, dynamo, died while driving.

Once the alternator fails the vehicle is running only on the battery, which will run out. Pull over safely while you still have lights and hazards.

This usually needs a replacement alternator or belt, which is often not a quick roadside job. A mechanic may be able to charge or swap the battery to get you to a workshop.

Keep one phone switched off as a reserve so you can still call for help later.
