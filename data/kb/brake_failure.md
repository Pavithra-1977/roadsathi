---
id: brake_failure
title: Brake failure or brake fluid loss
category: fault
---

Signs: pedal goes soft or to the floor, vehicle does not slow down properly, grinding, a brake warning light, fluid near a wheel. Words drivers use: brake nahi lag raha, brake fail, spongy pedal, not stopping.

If brakes fail while moving: shift down through the gears, use the handbrake gradually, and steer away from traffic.

Once stopped, do not drive the vehicle again, not even to the next exit. Brake faults are not something to test or patch at the roadside.

This is a tow job. The vehicle should go to a workshop on a tow or flatbed.
