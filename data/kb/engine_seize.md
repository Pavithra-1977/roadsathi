---
id: engine_seize
title: Suspected engine seizure
category: fault
---

Signs: a loud knocking noise, the engine stopping suddenly and refusing to turn, heavy white or blue smoke, the oil warning light. Words drivers use: engine jam, knocking, engine band, oil light.

Do not try to restart. Each attempt can make the damage worse.

This is not a roadside repair. The vehicle needs to be towed to a workshop for inspection. Use the waiting time to arrange safe onward travel for passengers.
