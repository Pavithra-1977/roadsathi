---
id: clutch_failure
title: Clutch failure
category: fault
---

Signs: gears will not engage, engine revs but the vehicle does not move (slipping), a burning smell, a pedal that stays down. Words drivers use: clutch gaya, gear nahi lag raha, clutch slip.

A failed clutch is usually not repairable at the roadside. It often needs the gearbox to be separated in a workshop.

Do not keep forcing gears. Wait in a safe place; the vehicle will normally need towing. A mechanic can confirm whether it is the clutch cable or fluid, which are sometimes simpler fixes.
