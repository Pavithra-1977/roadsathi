---
id: overheating
title: Engine overheating
category: fault
---

Signs: temperature needle in the red, steam from the bonnet, a sweet smell, warning light. Words drivers use: garam ho gaya, overheat, dhuan, steam, temperature high.

Pull over and switch the engine off. Continuing to drive can seriously damage the engine.

Never open the radiator cap or coolant reservoir while the engine is hot. The system is under pressure and hot coolant can cause serious burns. Wait at least 30 minutes.

Keep children away from the front of the vehicle. A mechanic will check coolant level, hoses, the fan and the belt before you drive on.
