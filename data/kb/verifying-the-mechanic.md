title: Verifying the mechanic who arrives
category: safety
---

Check that the person who arrives matches the name, photo and vehicle plate shown in the app.

RoadSathi gives you a four-digit arrival code. Read it out only when the mechanic is standing with you; work should not start until they enter it.

Do not hand over keys or documents to someone who cannot confirm the job in the app. If anything feels wrong, step away and call the number shown in the app, or 112.
